How to Use PhishGuard

A simple guide to analyzing your messages.

1

Input your Message

You can either type/paste the suspicious SMS directly into the text box, or upload a screenshot of the message from your phone. You can also drag and drop images directly into the box.

2

Scan & Analyze

Click the "Scan Messages Now" button. The system will extract the text, analyze the keywords, verify any detected links against global phishing databases, and determine the domain's risk level.

3

Review the Results

The system will return a classification: SAFE, SPAM, or SCAM. You will also see a detailed explanation of why the message was flagged. If you believe the system made a mistake, you can use the feedback buttons to alert our administrators.

Privacy & Data Policy

Transparency in how we handle your information.

At PhishGuard, we prioritize the ethical handling of data submitted for analysis. This system is developed primarily for academic research and threat intelligence gathering.

Data Collection

When you submit a text message or image for scanning, the content is securely transmitted to our servers for analysis. We collect the raw text, any extracted URLs, and the final classification label (Safe/Spam/Scam).

How We Use Your Data

The data collected is utilized to improve our local detection models and refine our system's accuracy. System Administrators have access to review submitted logs and user feedback to resolve conflicts (The Conflictor process) and retrain the system. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Sensitive Information Warning

Users are strictly advised not to submit messages containing Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as full names, banking PINs, OTPs, or passwords. PhishGuard is designed to analyze promotional, suspicious, or generic text structures.

Cookies & Local Storage

This website does not use tracking cookies for advertising. We strictly use client-side localStorage to remember your consent preferences and temporarily display your recent scans on your device. This local data is never shared with other users.

How to Install & Bypass Restrictions

Step 1: Open Google Play Store

Tap on your profile picture in the top right corner and select Play Protect.

Step 2: Open Play Protect Settings

Tap the gear icon in the top right corner to open the settings.

Step 3: Toggle off Scanning

Turn off the switch that says "Scan apps with Play Protect".

Step 4: Confirm Action

Tap "Turn off" or "Pause" when the warning popup appears.

Step 5: Verify it is Disabled

Make sure the toggle switch is completely greyed out.

Step 6: Install PhishGuard

Open the downloaded APK from your browser or file manager and click "Install".

Step 7: Go to App Settings

Go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Manage Apps > PhishGuard. Tap the three dots (or scroll to the bottom) and toggle "Allow restricted settings".

Step 8: Confirm Restrictions Allowed

Verify that the option is now toggled on and active.

Step 9: Open App and Set as Default

Open PhishGuard from your home screen. When prompted, select PhishGuard as your default SMS app to enable background scanning.
